MRI pipeline
01

Raw scans, real motion.

Patient movement during DCE-MRI acquisition is common, and it makes lesions hard to detect. Most pipelines just discard those scans.

02

Restored, not discarded.

Rigid and non-rigid co-registration, isotropic resampling, normalization. Dice score on high-motion cases improved from 47% to 67% after preprocessing.

03

Segmentation a model can trust.

An nnU-Net model trained for breast lesion segmentation runs on the corrected volumes, not the raw ones.

04

New hospitals, new scanners, no retraining from scratch.

A confidence-guided adaptation step lets the model learn from its own high-certainty predictions on new hospital data, with no new manual annotations required at either site.

05

The confidence numbers hold up.

Prediction confidence rose from 0.52 to 0.76 across an eleven-case validation cohort spanning two new hospitals.

06

Continuous monitoring, built in.

Scanner drift and data drift get tracked as new sites come online, so accuracy doesn't quietly decay over time.
