Data science built for clinical and pharmaceutical research

Nevyon DataLabs is a data and AI consultancy focused on turning complex clinical and research data into real-world evidence based predictions that support faster, more confident decision-making in pharmaceutical and healthcare research.

Work spans computational pathology and whole-slide imaging, medical imaging pipelines, and structured clinical data — built as production-ready MLOps infrastructure rather than research prototypes that never leave the lab. Every pipeline is designed with privacy-preserving principles at its core, so sensitive healthcare data stays protected throughout the analysis lifecycle.

At a glance

  • Computational pathology & whole-slide imaging (WSI)
  • Medical imaging — CT, MRI, radiology pipelines
  • Structured clinical & trial data
  • Privacy-preserving, GDPR-compliant infrastructure
  • Production MLOps, not research prototypes

Where the work happens

Four areas, from raw clinical data through to a decision a pharmaceutical or healthcare team can act on.

Pathology & imaging

Whole-slide imaging pipelines, digital pathology, and medical imaging (CT, MRI) — the data types that need domain-specific handling to be useful for AI.

AI & ML pipelines

Deep learning and domain adaptation methods applied to messy, real-world clinical data — not benchmark datasets.

Real-world evidence

Turning model output into evidence a research or clinical team can actually stand behind, with a clear line back to the underlying data.

MLOps & deployment

Cloud-based infrastructure that gets a pipeline from a working notebook to something a client's team can run in production.

Privacy by design

GDPR-compliant data handling built in from the first design decision, not bolted on before a compliance review.

Clinical data engineering

Getting the unglamorous data plumbing right — the part that determines whether everything downstream actually works.
