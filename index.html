Services

Where the work happens

Four areas, from raw clinical data through to a decision a pharmaceutical or healthcare team can act on.

Computational pathology & imaging

Whole-slide imaging pipelines, digital pathology, and medical imaging (CT, MRI) — data types that need domain-specific handling to become real-world evidence.

AI & ML pipelines

Deep learning and domain adaptation methods applied to messy, real-world clinical data — not benchmark datasets — to produce predictions that hold up.

Real-world evidence generation

Turning model output into actionable insight a research or clinical team can stand behind, with a clear line back to the underlying data.

MLOps & deployment

Cloud-based infrastructure that gets a pipeline from a working notebook to something a client's team can run in production.

Privacy-preserving infrastructure

GDPR-compliant data handling built in from the first design decision, not bolted on before a compliance review.

Clinical data engineering

Getting the unglamorous data plumbing right — the part that determines whether everything downstream actually works.

Approach

How an engagement runs

A straightforward path from a client's raw data to a decision they can act on.

1

Understand the data

Audit what's actually available — pathology slides, imaging, structured records — and what's realistic to build from it.

2

Build & validate

Develop the pipeline against real, messy clinical data, with privacy safeguards built in from the start, not added later.

3

Deploy & hand over

Ship production-ready infrastructure the client's own team can run, monitor, and maintain after the engagement ends.
